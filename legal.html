Privacy, terms and licences

These notes are written in plain English for the people who use this app. They are not legal advice, and the owner of this copy of the app is responsible for checking the rules that apply where they live.

Privacy

  • Where your data lives. Birth details, notes, chats, lessons and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). This app has no accounts, no database and no server of its own. You can export or delete everything under Settings.
  • The web host. The pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub can see that your device asked for the page (for example your IP address). The app owner does not receive or control that information. See GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Place search. If you press Search, the place name you typed is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find coordinates. Nothing else is sent. You can skip this by typing latitude, longitude and time zone yourself.
  • AI chat (optional). If you add your own key and ask a question, this app sends your question, your notes, your lessons, your birth year and the computed chart facts (including dasha dates, from which a birth date can be worked out) to the AI company you chose: Google, Groq or OpenRouter. Your name and birth place are never sent. The box "Facts sent to the AI" shows exactly what goes out. That company's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives.
  • Your AI key. It goes only from your browser to the company you chose. If you choose to save it, it is encrypted with your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM). If you forget the passphrase the key cannot be recovered; just add it again.
  • No tracking. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no fingerprinting.
  • Other people's details. Only enter birth details of another person if you have their permission, or if you are a parent or carer acting for them.
  • Age. This app is for adults (18 and over).

Terms of use

  • Purpose. For learning and self-reflection. The calculations are astronomical facts. The readings are traditional interpretations. They are not predictions that can be proven, and nobody can promise exact outcomes from a chart.
  • Not advice. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial, investment or psychological advice. Do not make health, money, legal, marriage or safety decisions because of this app. If you are in distress, please contact a trusted person, local emergency services or a helpline.
  • AI can be wrong. The AI explains the computed facts and may still make mistakes. Check what it says against the Chart, Dasha and Reading tabs.
  • No remedies for sale. The app does not sell or recommend gemstones, rituals or amulets and does not use fear to push any action.
  • Your own use. You are responsible for your AI key and for following the terms of the AI provider and the place-search service. Open-Meteo's free service is for non-commercial use, so do not add advertising or charge for this app while it uses that service.
  • No warranty. The app is provided "as is", without any promise that it is accurate, available or fit for a purpose. To the extent the law allows, the owner and contributors are not liable for loss arising from its use.
